Features

Probabilities before the match.
Results after it.

See what each prediction stands on, then judge it against what happened. Read how the model works and how to judge its record.

Match and score predictions
Home win, draw and away win probabilities for upcoming matches in the leagues we follow. Expected goals and the likely score are in the match detail.
A transparent model
A Poisson goal model with the Dixon-Coles correction for low scores. On featured matches it is blended with market consensus probabilities, 45% model and 55% market, the same weighting every time.
Every pick checked
Results shows the prediction made before kickoff next to the final score. The single pick and the double chance are scored separately, hits and misses alike.
Your teams
Pick the clubs you follow and see their matches first. Optional kickoff reminders and result notifications, and a home-screen widget for what's coming up.
Optional Premium
Model predictions and the weekly track record are free. Premium adds AI-assisted written analysis, squad news and the model and market side by side for featured matches.
Leagues in several countries
Leagues in several countries, including England, Germany, Spain, Italy, France and Türkiye. The Matches screen in the app shows the current fixtures.
How it works

Three steps to the numbers

Golmodel does the arithmetic, and shows its work.

01
Browse upcoming matches
See upcoming fixtures from every league we cover. Each row shows the pick, the 1 / X / 2 probabilities and the predicted score at a glance.
02
Dive into the analysis
Tap a match for the full breakdown: the model's probabilities, expected goals and the likely score; on featured matches also the market comparison, squad news and a written analysis.
03
Compare predictions with results
Open Results to compare finished matches with the predictions made before kickoff. Follow your teams and choose match reminders to return for their next fixture.

Questions about Golmodel

Is Golmodel free?

The statistical model predictions and weekly track record are free. Optional Premium adds AI-assisted analysis and extra context for selected matches.

Is Golmodel a betting app?

Golmodel is a football analysis app. It does not accept wagers or offer real-money betting. Predictions are informational and are not guarantees.

What does a score prediction mean?

It is one possible scoreline suggested by a probability model. The most likely scoreline can still have a low probability; other scores remain possible.

Which devices and languages are supported?

The App Store app supports iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app is available in English and Turkish.
