Features

Everything you need to
make better predictions

Built on a Dixon-Coles statistical model blended with market consensus probabilities. The figures shown here are a historical snapshot of 304 completed matches, not a live success rate or a guarantee of future results. Read how to interpret the model and its results.

📊
Dixon-Coles Model
Advanced Poisson model with Dixon-Coles correction for low-score matches. The correction accounts for dependence in low-score outcomes.
⚖️
Market Blend
45% statistical model, 55% market consensus. A fixed weighting, applied the same way to every match rather than tuned per fixture.
🏥
Squad Analysis
Real-time injury and suspension data automatically adjusts expected goal lambdas before computing probabilities.
🎯
Double Chance
73% accuracy on double chance calls across 304 completed matches. When the top two outcomes sit close together the model says so, instead of forcing a single pick.
⚡️
Harmony Score
✅ when the model and the market agree, ⚠️ when they diverge. A plain reading of how far apart the two sources are.
🌍
15 Leagues
Fifteen competitions across Europe, South America and Asia: England, Germany, Italy, Spain, France, Türkiye, Norway, Sweden, Finland, Denmark, Iceland, Brazil, South Korea, Vietnam, and the top European club competition.

Questions about Golmodel

Is Golmodel free?

The statistical model predictions and weekly track record are free. Optional Premium adds AI-assisted analysis and extra context for selected matches.

Is Golmodel a betting app?

Golmodel is a football analysis app. It does not accept wagers or offer real-money betting. Predictions are informational and are not guarantees.

What does a score prediction mean?

It is one possible scoreline suggested by a probability model. The most likely scoreline can still have a low probability; other scores remain possible.

Which devices and languages are supported?

The App Store app supports iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app is available in English and Turkish.
