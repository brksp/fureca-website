Support

We're here to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

Email support

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email us at golmodel.support@proton.me. We typically respond within 24-48 hours.

Report a bug

Found something that doesn't work as expected? Please describe what happened, what you expected, and your device and its iOS or Android version. Send details to golmodel.support@proton.me.

Feature requests

Have an idea for a new feature or improvement? We'd love to hear it. Send your suggestion to golmodel.support@proton.me.
